## The model
Dan hands most implementation to Claude, so the metric is **dollars per hour of Dan's attention**.
Ranked by that metric:
1. Fixed-price freelance jobs with crisp scope (Upwork, direct): highest, Claude does the build.
2. Low-touch salaried/contract roles (Djinni, small teams, async, part-time allowed): steady, and
   they stack. Two of them plus job1 clears the target.
3. Hourly freelance: fine, but pays by the clock.
4. A full EU role with meetings: by far the most money per month, but it consumes the attention
   budget and may bring exclusivity terms. Worth it if the number is €3,000+ gross.

## The ask (locked 2026-09-13)
- **Djinni profile:** $1,500/mo, 3 years experience, Vienna (Austria), remote, full-time + part-time.
- **Djinni per application:** 60-70% of the visible band, never the floor. No band: $1,500 for
  Ukrainian companies, $2,000 for EU/US product companies. **Floor $1,000** for a low-touch stacked
  role; below that only when hours are clearly ≤ 10/week. Never change a number mid-process.
- **Upwork:** $30-35/hr hourly; fixed-price at mid-budget, milestone-based (see `client-comms.md`).
- **EU employment (Vienna):** ask **€48-55k/year gross** (Austria pays 14 salaries; that is roughly
  €3,400-3,900 per month × 14). Junior/mid full-stack in Vienna sits in that range.
- **EU freelance/contract:** €35-45/hr, or €4,000-5,000/mo for a 4-day week.
- **Web3 (added 2026-09-15):** full-time remote junior/junior+ EVM role **$3,000-4,000/mo** (60-70% of a
  visible band, never the floor), **floor $2,500**; contract €35-45/hr; part-time stackable web3 roles
  keep the $1,500 floor; Ukrainian web3 companies $2,000 with no band. Claims come from `me/web3.md`.

## Stacking rules
- Read every contract for **exclusivity / non-compete / "full working time"** wording before
  signing. Ukrainian FOP contracts are usually non-exclusive; EU employment contracts usually are
  not. Ask plainly if unclear.
- Prefer roles that describe small teams, ownership, async work, "1-3 people per project".
- Cap the total: one daily call is fine, two are manageable, three is where it breaks.
- Keep job1 healthy. It is the floor that makes the hunt possible.

## Legal / money notes (not advice, things to check)
- Vertriebene ID card expected 2026-09-21: exempts from the Foreigners Employment Act, so Austrian

  becomes possible.
- Ukrainian FOP while living in Austria: after 183 days Dan is an Austrian tax resident; the
  UA-AT double-tax treaty applies. A Steuerberater consult before year end is cheap insurance.
- N26 is fine for salary; Upwork payouts also work with it.
