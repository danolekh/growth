# Client Communication & Pricing

How Dan runs a project once hired. This is what turns a first contract into 5-star reviews and
repeat work.

## Pricing
- **Quote mid-range of the stated budget.** (Oasi Kadir budget €1,000–1,500 → quoted $1,300.)
  Cheapest signals low quality; highest loses on price. Middle signals confident professional.
- **Milestone-based, deliverable-tied.** The **20 / 20 / 40 / 20** split across 4 milestones
  worked well: foundation, core build, main deliverables (biggest chunk), polish+deploy+handover.
- Each milestone has a **clear, verifiable deliverable** so the client knows exactly what they're
  approving and paying for.
- Offer a **bug-fix / buffer window** after the final milestone — low cost, high trust.

## Update cadence
- **Proactive update per milestone** — don't wait to be asked.
- **Short video / screen walkthroughs** (Loom-style or YouTube unlisted) showing what was built.
  Keep them short and the audio clean.
- **Written summary** alongside each video: bullet list of what's done, what's next, what you need.
- **Share working URLs** — Vercel preview, Strapi admin (with credentials), repo on request.
- **Flag blockers and ask for assets early** — booking embed URL, logo/brand assets, wireframes,
  real content. Make it easy for the client to unblock you, and make clear it's not blocking you
  when you can proceed with placeholder/seed data.

## Tone
- Warm, confident, concise. Peer-to-peer, not subservient.
- Acknowledge the client's strengths ("I love that you have a well-defined scope").
- Own mistakes lightly and move on (e.g. apologizing for video background noise, then fixing it).

## Handover (final milestone)
- README covering: local dev setup, deployment, how to add pages, how to add content types.
- Clean, documented repo. Commented components and API calls (clients explicitly value this).
- Offer a short handover call (e.g. 30 min).

## Upwork rules
- Keep all communication **on-platform** until a contract is active.
- Attach resume with **contacts stripped** ("Resume - no contacts.pdf").
- Don't share or solicit off-platform contact details in proposals.
