## English (send this one)

Hi! Two lines in your post describe my last year almost exactly: debugging plain JavaScript with no
framework to hide behind, and making conversion pages fast.

The clearest example. I rebuilt a client's site where the old version was WordPress with a minified
production bundle and no source code anywhere. I reverse-engineered the interactions straight out of
the shipped bundle, a parallax carousel, a card carousel and a menu animation, and reimplemented them
1:1 in the new build. The same job took mobile PageSpeed from 69 to 99 and load time from around 16
seconds to under 2, which for a conversion page is the whole ballgame. It closed with a 5.0 review and
there's a public case study at danolekh.com/p/oasi-kadir.

On the rest of the stack. Node and Express APIs, Docker, GitHub Actions for CI/CD, Linux, env config,
webhooks and async flows are daily tools for me. I built an e-commerce platform on Next.js with a
custom admin panel for 2000+ products and full multi-locale routing in IT and EN, so i18next and
locale URL handling are familiar territory. At Radency I worked on a multi-tenant React/Express system
with 10,000+ active users, and most recently I built Quextro, an ed-tech platform, solo from an empty
repo to real users, with Docker, CI/CD and OpenTelemetry around it.

Where I'd be ramping, and I'd rather say it now than in week one. I haven't owned production Nginx
configs, my apps have sat behind Vercel and Cloudflare rather than a reverse proxy I configured
myself, same story for DigitalOcean droplets and rsync deploys. On payments I've done Stripe Checkout
and webhooks, but not PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay or 3DS specifically. Linux and Docker I'm
comfortable in, so the infra side is a short ramp, and the payment edge cases are exactly the kind of
"the redirect came back with a state nobody planned for" work I like. Happy to do a test task if
that's useful.

CET hours are fine, I'm in Kyiv. Ukrainian is native, English is B2 and I've worked with clients in
Italy and the UK.
