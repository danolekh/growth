## Raw post (abridged)
Half the job is shipping in the framework itself; the other half is embedded with paying
customers deploying agents to production - in their Slack channels and on calls, writing code in
their repos, reviewing their PRs, working through architecture with their engineers. What's
learned there feeds back as bug fixes, features and new primitives in Mastra. One current
customer engineer is among the top bug-fixers in the whole codebase. Wants a full-stack
TypeScript engineer who has built agents and can explain technical decisions clearly to engineers
who haven't seen the code. Lots of EU customers and response-time SLAs, so US Eastern/Central or
EU hours work best. Process: call with the co-founder/CPO (leads customer engineering), a
conversation with a customer engineer, a 2-3 hour take-home, then presenting it for feedback.
