decide how features should work. Design APIs and backend services. Postgres schemas, queries,
migrations. Build and maintain real-time features such as chat. Integrate third parties for
payments, e-signatures, invoicing, email. Debug production issues. Review code.

Teams are 1-3 people per project. Sometimes you are the only engineer on a project.
